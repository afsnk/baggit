Baggit helps your business collect payments (both `FIAT{:sh}` and `STABLECOIN{:sh}`), settle users with payout directly to their bank account, onboard and manage user wallets with seamless KYB, compliant and bank grade wallet infrastructure with zero complexity and consistent uptime.

## Why Baggit?
Teams choose [Baggit](https://baggit.link) when they need to quickly go to market with a payment solution that unlocks global user access to their products and services, without owning complex bank rails, integrations or compliance workflows. You get a simple API with customizable widgets, while we handle routing, collections, settlement paths and the operational complexity behind the scenes.
